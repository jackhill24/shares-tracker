Welcome back

Your trades stay with you

The calculations run in this browser tab, and your trades, targets and settings are saved only in this browser. To look up prices, the page sends ticker symbols and date ranges, and nothing else, to a small price relay, which passes them on to Yahoo Finance.

Not financial advice. Every figure is an estimate and prices can be delayed or wrong. Check anything important against your broker.

The CSV format

One row per buy or sell, with these columns:

code
Ticker as your broker shows it: VAS, AAPL, BTC
exchange
ASX, NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE, HKEX, TSX, XMIL, CRYPTO (USD) or CRYPTO-AUD
date
2024-03-15 or 15/03/2024, day first
type
buy, sell or drp
quantity
Units, always positive
unit_price
Price per unit in the exchange's currency
platform
Your broker's name
fees
Optional. Brokerage, in the exchange's currency

Good to know

  • Values are in Australian dollars.
  • Only the exchanges listed under exchange are supported.
  • Numeric dates are read day first. Convert US-style MM/DD/YYYY dates first.
  • Share splits aren't handled yet. A holding that has split will show the wrong value.
  • There's no sector breakdown yet.
  • The first visit downloads about 25 MB, since Python runs in the page. Later visits load it from your browser's cache.
